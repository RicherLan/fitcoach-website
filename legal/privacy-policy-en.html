MIGO FIT (referred to as "we", "us" or "our") understands the importance of your personal information and is committed to collecting, using, storing and protecting it in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable data-protection laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) where they apply. Please read this Policy carefully before using the MIGO FIT App (the "Service" or "MIGO FIT").

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to your use of the MIGO FIT mobile application (iOS / Android) and all related products and services we provide.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: when you register or sign in via phone number, email, Google or Apple, we collect the corresponding account identifier (phone number, email, third-party account OpenID / UID) together with the nickname and avatar you configure.
  • Profile information: you may optionally provide gender, birthday, height, weight, fitness goals and similar information for personalised training recommendations. These fields are optional and you may modify or delete them at any time.
  • Workout records: exercise types, repetition counts, workout duration and similar data generated while you use the Service.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information: device model, operating-system version, app version, device language, screen resolution and device identifiers (such as IDFA / Android ID).
  • Log information: IP address, access time, crash logs, performance metrics used to improve service quality and troubleshoot issues.
  • Payment and membership information: when you subscribe, we collect the order ID, payment channel, order status, subscription plan and validity period. We do not store your bank-card number, credit-card number or the passwords of your Apple / Google / other payment accounts.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain and improve the core features of the Service (pose estimation, form correction, workout records);
  • Account identification, sign-in verification and security protection (including unusual sign-in alerts and single-device sign-in);
  • To deliver personalised training recommendations and membership services;
  • To process payments and orders;
  • Troubleshooting, log analysis and product optimisation;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Except in the following circumstances, we will not share your personal information with any third party:

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Storage location: your personal information is stored on compliant cloud infrastructure. Where required by applicable law, data relating to users in a particular region may be stored in that region.
  • Retention period: we retain your account information while your account is active. Following account deletion, personal data is deleted or anonymised within 30 business days unless a longer retention period is required by law.
  • Security measures: HTTPS in transit, encryption of sensitive fields at rest, principle-of-least-privilege access control, regular security reviews.
  • Data-breach notice: in the event of a personal-data incident, we will notify you and the competent supervisory authority in accordance with applicable law (including GDPR Art. 33/34 where relevant).

6. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the following rights in respect of your personal information:

  • Access and rectification: view and edit your profile from the "Me" section in the App;
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten"): delete your workout records; delete your account;
  • Withdraw consent: revoke permissions granted to the Service in your device settings;
  • Data portability: request a copy of the personal data you have provided in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format;
  • Restrict or object to processing: request that we restrict processing of your personal data in specific situations;
  • Do Not Sell / Share (CCPA): California residents may request that we do not sell or share their personal information. MIGO FIT does not sell personal information as defined by CCPA;
  • Account deletion: apply via "Account Settings → Delete Account" in the App or by emailing 1348270542@qq.com;
  • Complaints: contact us via section 10 below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

If you believe we may have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
